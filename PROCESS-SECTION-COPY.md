## Heading

Our Patient-centred Process


## Subhead

Four steps from first assessment to ongoing review — each one handled by the same multidisciplinary team.


## Steps


### 01. Tell Us About Your Pain

Complete our short pain assessment so we can better understand your symptoms, pain history, and treatment goals before your consultation.


### 02. Meet With Our Team

You’ll speak with one of our Pain Management Coordinators who will review your medical history, discuss previous treatments, and assess your condition in detail.


### 03. Receive Your Personalised Plan

After confirmation of treatment, we’ll develop a tailored multidisciplinary treatment plan based on your symptoms, lifestyle, existing health conditions, and long-term goals. Prescriptions will be delivered to your doorstep.


### 04. Ongoing Support & Review

Chronic pain changes over time. We’ll continue to review your progress and adjust your monthly script as needed to help you achieve the best possible outcomes.
