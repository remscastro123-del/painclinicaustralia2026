AHPRA-registered Doctors for Chronic Pain

Complex pain needs a doctor who has the whole picture.

Chronic pain is rarely just one symptom with one simple cause. It can involve multiple conditions, changing symptoms, long medical histories, and care from several practitioners.

At Pain Clinic Australia, our AHPRA-registered doctors form the medical backbone of your personalised pain care plan.

They bring your symptoms, medical history, current medicines, previous care, and wider health picture together. This helps your PCA team make informed decisions as your needs change.

When Pain Has a Long History

You shouldn’t have to compress years of pain into a rushed appointment.

Some patients come to us after years of specialist appointments, conflicting explanations, and incomplete relief despite ongoing care. Others are still living with pain after the original injury or condition has already been addressed.

Your medical history can become complicated quickly when symptoms persist. Our doctors take the time to understand what has already been tried, what helped, what didn’t, and what has changed since.

Step 1

Reviewing your complete pain history

Your doctor will explore how your pain began, how it has progressed, and how it affects your daily life. Previous diagnoses, investigations, procedures, and clinical recommendations can also provide valuable context.

Step 2

Looking at what you’ve already tried

You may have already seen several doctors or specialists before reaching Pain Clinic Australia. Understanding your previous care helps your doctor avoid looking at your pain as though you are starting from zero.

Step 3

Considering your current medicines

Your doctor can review the medicines you currently take alongside your wider medical history. This includes considering potential interactions, side effects, and other factors relevant to your ongoing care.

Step 4

Connecting changing symptoms

Chronic pain doesn’t always stay the same. New symptoms, changing pain patterns or shifts in your overall health may affect what needs attention next.

Step 5

Listening to your experience

A scan, test result or diagnosis doesn’t always capture the full impact of persistent pain. Our consultations are designed to give you room to explain what you’re experiencing and how it has changed your life.

What Happens During Your Consultation?

Your first consultation is about understanding the whole medical picture.

Your doctor will review your current symptoms, relevant health history, and previous care before discussing the next steps. Your consultation may include:

  • Discussing where your pain is located and how it behaves
  • Reviewing how long your symptoms have been present
  • Exploring diagnoses and underlying health conditions
  • Looking at previous approaches and their outcomes
  • Reviewing current medicines and potential interactions
  • Discussing side effects or tolerability concerns
  • Considering changes in symptoms over time
  • Looking at how pain affects sleep, movement, mood and daily function
  • Identifying areas that may need input from another member of your care team
  • Planning appropriate medical follow-up

Your PMC Keeps Things Moving

Good chronic pain care doesn’t stop when the consultation ends.

Your Pain Management Coordinator (PMC) helps keep the different parts of your care connected between appointments.

Regular check-ins give you an opportunity to discuss symptoms, changes, and concerns as they arise. Relevant updates can then be shared with your clinical team so your care doesn’t rely on you remembering everything at your next appointment.

The goal is continuity; not starting from scratch at every appointment.
